6. Times and date-times
   09:30
     chrono  09:30:00                    always has seconds
     UTV     09:30                       no seconds given, none shown
     UTV     09:30:00
   23:30 + 2 hours
     chrono  2026-11-06 01:30:00
     UTV     2026-11-06T01:30            wall-clock hours, into the next day

7. Parsing with offsets
   Z vs +00:00
     chrono  2026-11-05 14:30:00 +00:00  from ...Z
     chrono  2026-11-05 14:30:00 +00:00  from ...+00:00: the same value
     UTV     2026-11-05T14:30:00Z        an instant: UTC known, local offset not
     UTV     2026-11-05T14:30:00+00:00   a wall time at offset 0

8. Time zones

9. DST gap
   02:30 on 8 March 2026 in New York
     chrono  None                        you choose what to do
     UTV     no such time                2026-03-08T02:30 is skipped in America/New_York (gap)
     UTV     2026-03-08T03:30:00-04:00[America/New_York]
                                         Gap::ShiftForward

10. DST overlap
   01:30 on 1 November 2026 in New York
     chrono  Ambiguous(2026-11-01T01:30:00EDT, 2026-11-01T01:30:00EST)
     chrono  2026-11-01 01:30:00 EDT     .earliest()
     UTV     2 instants
             2026-11-01T01:30 occurs twice in America/New_York (ambiguous)
     UTV     2026-11-01T05:30:00Z
     UTV     2026-11-01T06:30:00Z
     UTV     2026-11-01T05:30:00Z        Overlap::Earliest

12. Durations vs periods
   One day vs 86,400 seconds across DST
     chrono  2026-11-01 12:00:00 EST     a calendar day
     chrono  2026-11-01 11:00:00 EST     86,400 seconds
     UTV     2026-11-01T12:00[America/New_York]
                                         a calendar day
     UTV     2026-11-01T11:00:00-05:00[America/New_York]
                                         86,400 POSIX seconds
     UTV     unsupported (needs-leap-seconds)
                                         SI seconds need a leap-second table
